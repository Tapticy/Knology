Unmetered bandwidth means your hosting provider does not charge you based on the amount of data transferred to and from your server.

For example, if your website gets:

100 GB of traffic this month,

1 TB next month,

5 TB the month after,


you won't receive overage charges just because you transferred more data.

However, unmetered does not mean unlimited speed or unlimited capacity. Most providers still limit the port speed of your server, such as:

100 Mbps unmetered – your connection can transmit up to 100 megabits per second.

1 Gbps unmetered – up to 1 gigabit per second.


So, you can transfer as much data as that connection speed allows over time, without paying per gigabyte.

A simple analogy:

Metered bandwidth is like paying for every liter of water you use.

Unmetered bandwidth is like paying a flat monthly fee for a water pipe of a certain size. You can use as much water as the pipe can deliver, but the pipe's size limits the maximum flow.
